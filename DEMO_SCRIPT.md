# 3-Minute Judge Demo

## 0:00 — Hook
“Digital identity is becoming impossible to trust by sight or sound alone. SENTINEL combines independent evidence sources into one explainable trust decision.”

## 0:20 — Authentic case
Upload authentic demo media.

Show:
- identity match
- media signals
- provenance
- Trust Score ~90+

## 0:55 — Trigger attack simulation
Click `RUN ATTACK SIMULATION`.

Show:
- changed media
- altered signals
- timeline

## 1:30 — Explainability
Open “WHY FLAGGED?”

Point to 2–3 concrete findings.

## 2:00 — Provenance
Show the provenance panel and explain:
“Missing provenance doesn't mean fake. It means we have less evidence.”

## 2:20 — Live challenge
If implemented, issue a simple liveness challenge and show the identity check.

## 2:45 — Close
“SENTINEL does not pretend one detector can know the truth. It combines identity, forensic, synchronization and provenance evidence, then tells a human exactly why the media deserves trust or review.”
